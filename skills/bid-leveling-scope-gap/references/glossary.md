# Glossary (bid leveling terms)

Short definitions for terms that appear in bid documents. For reference only.

- **Inclusion** — Work the bidder states is part of the bid price.
- **Exclusion** — Work the bidder states is not part of the bid price.
- **Allowance** — A stated amount included in the bid for a defined item whose final cost is not yet known.
- **Qualification** — A clarification or condition the bidder attaches to an item.
- **Addendum** — A formal change to the bid documents issued before bid submission.
